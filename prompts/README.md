# Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for AI assistants and coding agents, generated from https://appfinder.tech/prompts. Each works on its own: a role, inputs
to fill in, hard rules (never guess, name the source), an exact output format, and a fallback that needs no MCP. They are
starting points: check the result.

## Choosing software

- [Shortlist software for a job, with sources](./shortlist-software.md): Describe the job, team size and budget; get a three-app shortlist with what each one meets, misses or doesn't say.
- [Replace a tool you pay for](./replace-a-tool.md): Find real alternatives to a tool, what you'd lose by switching, and what buyers who switched chose.
- [Audit your software stack for overlap and cost](./audit-my-stack.md): Paste your tool list; get what overlaps, what to cut, and a cheaper replacement for each, with the monthly saving.
- [Decide between two tools](./compare-two-tools.md): Give your priorities and weights; get a decision matrix built from sourced facts, not marketing pages.

## Building and listing software

- [List your app on AppFinder from your coding agent](./list-my-app.md): The agent reads your repo, drafts only true facts, and prepares your free listing. You review and submit.
- [Write listing copy that passes review](./write-listing-copy.md): Tagline, description and features in the shape that gets approved: specific, factual, no superlatives.
- [Verify ownership of your listing with your coding agent](./verify-ownership.md): Give the agent your verification token; it adds the file or meta tag to your site and checks it is live.
- [Make your site readable by AI assistants](./ai-readable-site.md): Audit and fix llms.txt, robots.txt for AI crawlers, structured data, a plain-text pricing page and Markdown pages.
- [Make your pricing page answer-ready](./pricing-page-for-answers.md): Rewrite your pricing page so people and AI assistants quote it correctly: plain text plans, limits and a date.
- [Pre-launch SEO audit your agent can act on](./prelaunch-seo-audit.md): Find the thin pages, duplicate titles, missing canonicals and weak internal links before launch, then fix them.
- [Add programmatic SEO pages without creating spam](./safe-programmatic-seo.md): Generate comparison, alternatives or "best X for Y" pages only from real facts, with size limits and noindex rules.
- [See what buyers in your category ask for](./market-research-for-your-category.md): Use AppFinder's anonymous demand data to find the features and budgets buyers ask for and which apps answer them.
